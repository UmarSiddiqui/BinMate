Brand Essence

What BinMate stands for

BinMate is the app that quietly makes Perth life smoother. Not flashy. Not trying to be your best friend. Just reliably, calmly useful — like a good neighbour who leaves a note on your door: "Recycling goes out tonight."

Mission

Never miss a bin day again.

Simple as that. Every Perth household has missed a bin day. Every time it happens, you feel mildly stupid and have to wait another week. BinMate fixes that permanently, for $1.49 a month.

Position

The Perth-first utility app.

Not national. Not generic. Not a "bin reminder widget." BinMate knows Perth's 30 councils, WA's public holidays, the difference between Week A and Week B, and when your verge collection is.

Promise

Set it up once. Forget about it.

The app works silently in the background. It notifies you. It adjusts for public holidays. It handles FIFO schedules. You never have to think about bins again.

Brand Personality

Reliable Dry Local Calm Practical Confident Straight-talking Unfussy A little bit funny Perth-proud

Brand Archetype

The Reliable Neighbour

Not the tech bro. Not the life coach. The neighbour who's lived in Scarborough for 15 years, knows exactly when the yellow bin goes out, and just tells you — no app subscription pitch, no upsell. Just useful information, delivered on time.

BinMate speaks like that neighbour. Warm, dry, knows Perth, never tries too hard.

Colour Usage Rules

Lime — primary action only. CTA buttons, selected states, active indicators, links. Never use for decorative purposes. If everything is lime, nothing is.

Amber — verge collection alerts, "coming up soon" states, warning notices. Warm urgency.

Red — only for errors, missed collections, or destructive actions. Never for decoration.

Teal — informational states, tips, onboarding hints. Calm and secondary.

Voice & Tone

How BinMate speaks

Dry. Direct. A little bit Perth. We don't perform helpfulness — we just are helpful. We never explain ourselves twice. We never apologise for being a bin app. We're comfortable in our lane.

Pillar 01

Plain-spoken

No jargon. No marketing fluff. "Yellow bin tonight" is better than "Your recycling collection service is scheduled for this evening." Perth people are direct. So is BinMate.

Pillar 02

Dry wit

We're allowed one eyebrow raise. Not comedy, not memes — just the mild satisfaction of a well-timed understatement. "Bins out tonight. You know the drill." That's enough.

Pillar 03

Earned confidence

We know Perth. We know the councils. We don't say "we think" or "this might be." Either we know it or we don't show it. Confidence is quiet, not loud.

Why this pricing works

$1.49 is psychologically unthreatening

It's less than a coffee. Less than a parking meter. It doesn't require a conversation. People approve recurring payments at this price without asking anyone's permission. Cancellation friction exceeds the payment friction — most users just keep it.

Annual is the real money-maker

$9.99/year feels like a "deal" vs $1.49/month. Crucially, annual subscribers churn at 3–5x lower rates than monthly. Target 40% annual uptake. At 5,000 users with 40% annual: ~$26k/year from annual + ~$54k/year from monthly = ~$80k gross. After Apple's 15%: ~$68k net.

Free tier drives installs, not revenue

Free tier exists to reduce install friction — particularly for new Perth arrivals who aren't sure if they need the app. The 7-day trial does the conversion. Free tier is permanently hobbled (no push, no holiday shifts) so upgrading is obvious, not coerced.

What we will never do

✕

Ads. No. A bin reminder app showing ads is a broken trust statement. BinMate is ad-free, forever.

✕

Sell location data. Your address never leaves your device except to resolve your council zone. We don't build profiles. We don't work with data brokers.

✕

Freemium dark patterns. No fake countdowns. No "your data will be deleted" threats. No guilt-trip paywalls. Just a clean offer at a fair price.

✕

Enterprise bloat. BinMate stays a simple consumer iOS app. We will not pivot to "smart waste management solutions for municipalities."
